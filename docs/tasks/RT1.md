Facts (measured):
- PCSX2 level select (refs/pcsx2_dumps/ref_levelselect) draws the TV preview from THREE textures: tbp 0x2e40 (256x256 PSMCT32), 0x3240 (256x256 PSMCT32), 0x3640 (256x128 PSMT8). In ours (PS2X_RD_TEXSAVE, logs/texdump/*.bmp) 0x2e40 and 0x3240 are completely BLACK in the VRAM the renderer decodes (0 non-black pixels), 0x3640 holds the real preview picture. 0x2e40/32 = fbp 0x172, 0x3240/32 = fbp 0x192: they are almost certainly render targets (the game draws into FRAME fbp 0x172/0x192 and then samples them as textures, a CRT/blur style pass). HYPOTHESES C446 says previews are black and become pixel garbage after long play.
- PCSX2 pause screen (ref_pause): frame starts with a copy of the display buffer (tex 0x0 512x512, fb=0x70 scratch), then draws that shrunk into fb=0xe0 (256x224, tbp 0x1c00), then draws tex 0x1c00 back into fb 0x0 with FIX alpha blends; ours shows a black pause screen.
