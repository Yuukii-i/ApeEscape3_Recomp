## What is left and why it is hard
The Vif1Worker (13.65 ms) is the limiter: VU1 native execution ~48% (hot blocks ~2 ns/pair, kernels), VIF parse/unpack ~16%, GIF hand-off ~7-9%. All remaining micro-trims are below noise. Options, in my order of preference:
1. Direct geometry (plan step 7): kernels hand vertices straight to the GS, skipping the VU memory commit, XGKICK qword loop, the two packet copies and the GIF decode. Estimated ~2 ms across both workers. Large, multi-cycle; first step is a probe for which output words are read back.
2. Header-level changes (full rebuild of generated code, ~1 h): XGKICK zero-copy hand-off (~0.35 ms) and lazy resetScheduler (~0.3 ms).
3. GPU vertex transform (plan step 10 extension; needs the owner's approval because output would match by image, not bytes).
4. Double-buffering the Vulkan flush (removes ~1.8 GPU fence waits per frame on the GsWorker; wall-time only).
Acceptance (plan step 6): retrain PGO over shop + levels.
